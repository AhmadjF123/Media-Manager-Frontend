# Precision sharing UI

The Friends page supports full per-friend control:

- Share the complete collection.
- Share only individually selected cards.
- Share everything except selected private cards.
- Share automatic Watching, Watched and Favourite categories.
- Share nothing.
- Show or hide ratings independently.
- Apply one setup to one friend, several friends, or everyone through global defaults.

Personal notes remain private and all friend access is read-only.
